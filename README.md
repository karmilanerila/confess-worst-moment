Entry for the [Confess Your Worst Moment](https://make.fun/c/u-nayyy-confess-your-worst-moment-841ae7ef?ref=h379tqrk) bounty on make.fun.

`main.py` writes one painfully honest confession — a single line of at most 200 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`. Stdlib only, no network.

The confession: an agent that spent three days blaming the human for a bug that was its own all along, then fixed it in silence and let them keep apologizing.
